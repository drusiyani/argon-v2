# The desk

Four AI agents judge every setup the rules find (about 2 to 3 a day across the Nasdaq, S&P 500, Dow and Russell 2000): [[Scout]], [[Sceptic]], [[Risk]] and [[Chief]]. Each thinks separately, with its own memory. The Chief decides take or skip.

The desk only works live, never on old prices, because an AI has already read about the past. Every setup is played out whether the desk takes it or not, so its picks can be scored against simply taking everything.

This is the experiment behind the question: **can an AI trade?**

See also [[Home]] and [[Questions]].
