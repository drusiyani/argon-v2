# Sweep

Price pokes just past a [[Key levels|key level]] and comes straight back. The idea is that big players pushed price there on purpose to trigger everyone's stop orders, then reversed.

If price pushes through and *stays* there instead, it's a breakout, not a sweep, and the strategy doesn't trade it. Only sweeps during [[Trading hours]] count. The candle that did the poking is a [[Big push candle]].
