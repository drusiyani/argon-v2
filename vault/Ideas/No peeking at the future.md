# No peeking at the future

A common mistake in testing: using information that wasn't available yet at the time. An early version of [[The order-block strategy]] used the hourly trend before the hour had finished. It looked like it made +20.7R. After fixing it, it lost money. See the [[Changelog]]. Now everything waits for candles to finish first.
