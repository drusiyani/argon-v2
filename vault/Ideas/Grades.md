# Grades

Every setup gets a score out of 100 *before* we know how it turns out: level quality 25, [[SMT]] 15, how big the trend-flip candle was 20, hourly trend agreeing 15, [[Buying low, selling high]] 10, time of day 10, gap and order block overlapping 5. A++ is 85 or more, A+ 72, A 60, B 45, otherwise C. If higher grades don't actually win more, the [[Edge report]] will say so.
