# The order-block strategy

A second, simpler strategy, kept separate from [[The main strategy]]. It buys or sells the first time price comes back to a fresh 15-minute [[Order block]] during [[Trading hours]], as long as the hourly trend agrees. Stop just past the block, target twice the risk or the next level.
