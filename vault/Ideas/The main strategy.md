# The main strategy

The main strategy has a list of checks, in order. Every one must pass:

1. An important [[Key levels|level]] to target
2. A [[Sweep]] during [[Trading hours]], not a breakout
3. Matches the day's direction, when that check is switched on
4. The other market disagrees ([[SMT]]), when that check is switched on
5. A [[Trend flip]] within 40 minutes
6. Price comes back to the [[Price gap (FVG)|gap]] or [[Order block]] within an hour
7. It passes the risk rules from Argon v1

The stop goes just past the sweep. The target is the next level on the other side, if it's worth at least twice the risk. Anything that fails a check is a [[Skipped trades|skipped trade]]. The full rules are in [[Rulebook v0.8]].
