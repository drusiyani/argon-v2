# Trend flip

The trend changes direction with one big, decisive candle. This is one of the checks in [[The main strategy]]: after a [[Sweep]], the trend has to flip within 40 minutes. A fast move like this usually leaves a [[Price gap (FVG)]] or an [[Order block]] behind, which becomes the entry.
