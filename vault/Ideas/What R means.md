# What R means

Results are measured in R, meaning "times the amount risked". If you risk £500 and win £1,000, that's +2R. If you lose the £500, that's −1R. Each trade risks 0.5% of the account, so +1R adds 0.5%. "After costs" takes off a small amount (0.05R) per trade for fees.
