# SMT

The Nasdaq and the S&P 500 normally move together, and so do gold and silver. If the Nasdaq makes a new high but the S&P 500 doesn't, they disagree. Traders call this SMT, and it's a hint the move is fake and about to reverse.

It's one of the checks in [[The main strategy]]. The [[Edge report]] tests whether it actually helps.
