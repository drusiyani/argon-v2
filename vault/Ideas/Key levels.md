# Key levels

Highs and lows on the chart where lots of traders have probably left stop orders. The big ones are [[Session levels]]: yesterday's high and low, last week's, and the Asia and London ranges. When two highs (or lows) are almost equal, even more stops pile up there.

The [[The main strategy|main strategy]] only cares about the important ones. When price pokes past one and comes back, that's a [[Sweep]].
