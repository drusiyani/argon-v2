Can an AI trade?

Argon v2 is a pre-registered paper-trading research project on Nasdaq futures, run by Dru Siyani. Four simple rules find trades next to a Nasdaq holding. Every weekday at 8:45 New York, four AIs (Claude, GPT, Gemini and Kimi) say how likely a trend day is and how big the book should be. Every result goes in a hash-chained ledger that nobody can quietly change.

The main book

The plain package: a Nasdaq holding plus every rules trade at 0.5% risk. Backtest after costs, mostly in-sample: 2021-26 +56.4% a year, worst drop -19.4%; 2010-20 +21.6% a year, worst drop -23.5%. Live results are expected to be well below these.

The rules freeze on 31 January 2027. Race 1 runs on paper from February to December 2027. Paper trading only, not financial advice. Read the pre-registration.
