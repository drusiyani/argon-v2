What this site does

Argon v2 tests a trading strategy built around one idea: big players push price past obvious highs and lows to trigger other traders' stop orders, then reverse. It trades the same markets as Argon v1's Kronos indices bot (Nasdaq, S&P 500, Dow and Russell 2000 futures). The site replays real prices and records every time the strategy would have bought, sold or stayed out, and why. Nothing trades for real. The goal is to find out, before it goes live on 1 January, whether the strategy actually works.
